Who we are

More than a team.
A whole arena.

SF Esports Arena is a multi-game esports organization focused on building a connected competitive ecosystem. From tournaments and team development to community initiatives and esports content, our goal is to create meaningful opportunities for gamers at every level.

Born from a passion for gaming, we're building a platform where people can play, compete, grow and take their next step in esports.
